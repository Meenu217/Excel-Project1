Coffee Analytics: Interactive Coffee Sales Dashboard :
A dynamic, interactive data visualization tool built in Excel to explore global coffee sales performance—focusing on revenue over time, regional market comparisons, customer segments, and product preferences.

Short Description / Purpose :
The Coffee Sales Dashboard is a visually engaging and analytical Excel report designed to help stakeholders explore and analyze multi-regional coffee bean transactions. The dashboard focuses on tracking major sales drivers such as revenue trends by coffee type, country-wise performance, top-performing customers, and filterable product attributes (roast type, packaging size, and loyalty card status). This tool is intended for use by sales analysts, business strategists, and decision-makers who seek to understand purchasing trends and characteristics in the global coffee market.

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:
- 🟢 Microsoft Excel – Main data analytics and spreadsheet platform used for end-to-end report creation.
- 🔄 Data Transformation & Lookups – Advanced formulas including XLOOKUP and INDEX/MATCH used to consolidate orders, customer data, and product details.
- 📊 Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts – Utilized for data aggregation, metric calculations, and dynamic visual generation (line and bar charts).
- 🎛️ Interactive Slicers & Timeline – Connected slicers (Roast Type, Size, Loyalty Card) and an interactive Date Timeline enabling real-time cross-filtering   across all visual elements.
- 📋 Excel Tables (Ctrl + T) – Dynamic table structuring ensuring seamless data refresh and automatic range expansion.

Key Features & Functionality :
- End-to-End Data Pipeline: Gathered raw order data, cleaned records, and performed multi-table lookups to assemble a unified data model.
  
Dynamic Visualizations:
- Total Sales Over Time: Line chart tracking revenue splits across four coffee types (Arabica, Excelsa, Liberica, Robusta).
- Sales by Country: Bar chart highlighting regional performance across markets like the U.S., UK, and Ireland.
- Top 5 Customers: Filtered bar chart showcasing highest-value buyers.
